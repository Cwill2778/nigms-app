## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

### 2.1 Service Tiers

Client agrees to subscribe to one of the following maintenance tiers:

**☐ Essential Tier — $99/month per property**
- Bi-annual preventative maintenance (2 scheduled visits per year)
- Seasonal services: gutter cleaning, HVAC filter replacement, smoke/CO detector battery replacement
- Annual visual property inspection (exterior and interior)
- Standard tenant work order portal (labor billed at standard hourly rate; materials billed separately)
- One (1) annual property management report

**☐ Proactive Tier — $199/month per property**
- All Essential Tier services, PLUS:
- Quarterly preventative maintenance (4 scheduled visits per year)
- Two (2) hours of general handyman labor included monthly
- Priority tenant work order portal with guaranteed 48-hour response/diagnosis
- Two (2) property management reports per year

**☐ Comprehensive Tier — $399/month per property**
- All Essential and Proactive Tier services, PLUS:
- Monthly preventative check-ins (12 scheduled visits per year)
- Five (5) hours of general handyman labor included monthly
- Emergency dispatch with guaranteed 24-hour priority response
- Trade coordination services (see Section 2.4)
- Four (4) property management reports per year

### 2.3 Exclusions — Services NOT Included

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the following are NOT included in any tier:

- Licensed electrical work requiring permits
- Licensed plumbing work requiring permits
- Structural repairs or modifications
- Roofing replacement (inspections are included; repairs are not)
- Pest control or extermination
- Landscaping, lawn care, or tree removal
- Cosmetic upgrades or remodeling
- Appliance replacement (repair attempts are included under labor hours)
- Work requiring specialized licensing not held by Provider
- Any work on properties not listed in Section 2.2

### 2.4 Trade Coordination (Comprehensive Tier Only)

For issues requiring licensed specialists (HVAC replacement, electrical, major plumbing), Provider will:
1. Diagnose the issue
2. Identify and dispatch a qualified, licensed trade partner
3. Oversee the work and ensure quality standards are met
4. Communicate progress to the Owner

The Owner is responsible for paying the trade partner's invoice directly. Provider does not markup trade partner invoices. Provider is not liable for the work performed by third-party trade partners.

### 2.5 Definition of "General Handyman Labor"

Included labor hours cover tasks such as:
- Minor plumbing (running toilets, faucet replacement, caulking)
- Drywall patching and repair
- Door/window hardware adjustment
- Light fixture replacement
- Minor carpentry (shelving, trim, hinges)
- Painting (touch-up, not full rooms)
- General property upkeep and small repairs

Labor hours are measured as on-site work time only. Travel time to and from the property is not deducted from included hours.

## 4. INCLUDED LABOR HOURS

### 4.1 Non-Rollover Policy

Included labor hours (2 hours for Proactive; 5 hours for Comprehensive) do not accumulate or roll over to subsequent months. Unused hours expire at the end of each monthly billing cycle.

### 4.2 Scheduling

Labor hours are subject to scheduling availability. Client should request service with reasonable advance notice (minimum 48 hours for non-emergency requests). Provider will make best efforts to accommodate urgent requests within the response time guaranteed for Client's tier.

## 5. ACCESS & SCHEDULING

### 5.1 Property Access

Client and/or tenant must provide reasonable access to the property for all scheduled and requested services. This includes:
- Providing keys, access codes, or lockbox information
- Ensuring pets are secured during service visits
- Clearing work areas of personal belongings as needed

### 5.2 Missed Appointments

If Provider arrives for a scheduled visit and cannot access the property due to Client/tenant unavailability:
- The visit counts as completed for scheduling purposes
- The visit does not roll over or generate a credit
- Provider will attempt to reschedule within the same billing period if schedule permits

### 5.3 Provider Rescheduling

Provider may reschedule visits due to:
- Severe weather or unsafe conditions
- Emergency situations
- Provider illness

Provider will notify Client as soon as possible and reschedule within a reasonable timeframe.

## 6. EMERGENCY SERVICES

### 6.1 Definition of Emergency

An "emergency" is defined as a situation requiring immediate attention to prevent:
- Active water damage (burst pipe, flooding)
- Complete loss of heating in winter months (below 40°F)
- Gas leak or carbon monoxide detection
- Electrical hazard posing fire risk
- Security compromise (broken door/window lock)

### 6.2 Emergency Response

- **Essential Tier:** Emergency service available at premium hourly rate ($__/hour) plus materials
- **Proactive Tier:** Emergency service available at standard hourly rate plus materials
- **Comprehensive Tier:** Emergency dispatch within 24 hours; labor deducted from included hours first, then billed at standard rate

### 6.3 Limitations

Provider is NOT an emergency response service. For life-threatening emergencies (fire, gas explosion, structural collapse), Client/tenant must call 911 first. Provider is not liable for delays in emergency response due to scheduling, travel, or circumstances beyond our control.

Provider is not liable for:

- Delays caused by material shortages, supply chain issues, or third-party contractors

### 7.2 Indemnification

Client agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Provider from any claims, damages, or expenses arising from:
- Hazardous materials present on the property (lead, asbestos, mold)
- Injuries to third parties caused by pre-existing property conditions

- Client's failure to disclose known property defects

### 7.3 Workmanship Guarantee

Provider guarantees workmanship on all completed repairs for a period of 30 days from completion. If the same repair fails within 30 days due to Provider's workmanship (not materials, tenant misuse, or external factors), Provider will re-perform the repair at no additional labor cost.

### 10.1 Communication

Provider may communicate directly with tenant(s) for scheduling purposes. All decisions regarding repairs, expenditures, or property modifications require Owner approval unless:
- The repair falls within included labor hours AND costs less than $50 in materials
- It is an emergency as defined in Section 6.1

### 10.2 Landlord-Tenant Disputes

Provider is not a party to any landlord-tenant dispute. Provider will not mediate, arbitrate, or take sides in disputes between Owner and tenant. If a dispute interferes with Provider's ability to perform services, Provider may suspend services until the dispute is resolved.

### 10.3 Work Order Approval Threshold

- **Essential/Proactive Tier:** Owner approval required for any work order exceeding $100 in estimated cost (labor + materials)
- **Comprehensive Tier:** Owner approval required for any work order exceeding $200 in estimated cost
