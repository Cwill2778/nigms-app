## Notes

- Always upload a photo with your post (even a phone photo of the job site works)
- Keep posts under 300 words — Google truncates long posts
- Use the CTA button every time — "Call now" is usually best for local services
- Posts expire after 7 days on GBP, so consistency matters more than perfection
- After posting, share the same content on Nextdoor for double exposure
